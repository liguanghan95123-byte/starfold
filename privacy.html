Starfold Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Starfold is a single-player merge game made by IM Games. This policy explains the little the app does with information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, and language are saved only in local storage on your device. The app does not make an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not upload your save to us.

Advertising

The Android app shows optional rewarded videos through Google AdMob. If you choose to watch one, Google may receive an advertising identifier and technical data such as device type and IP address, under Google’s privacy policy. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings. The game does not require an ad to play. If an ad does not load, you simply do not get the optional bonus.

What we do not collect

We do not collect personal information, precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone audio. The app does not include analytics accounts or social login.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the developer contact listed on the Google Play store page.

更新日期：2026年11月26日

存在你设备上的内容

进度、设置和语言只保存在你的设备本地。我们不注册账号，不索取姓名或邮箱，也不把存档上传给我们。

广告

安卓版可以通过 Google AdMob 播放可选的激励视频。如果你选择观看，Google 可能收到广告标识符以及设备类型、IP 地址等技术信息，详见 Google 隐私政策。你可以在系统设置里限制广告 ID。不看广告也能正常游玩。广告没有加载时，只是拿不到那份额外奖励。

我们不收集的内容

我们不收集个人身份信息、精确位置、通讯录、照片或麦克风。应用不含分析账号或社交登录。
